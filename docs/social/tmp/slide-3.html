The solution
Two keys.
Free.
Fn
show / hide
⌘ D
start / stop

Fn shows or hides a tiny ChatGPT window.

Cmd+D starts and stops dictation on the real ChatGPT composer.

The transcript lands on your clipboard automatically. Cmd+V, done.

Free — it uses your own ChatGPT account, not a paid transcription API.
